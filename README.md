
MiniCraft — my own game! like minecraft

A 3D sandbox survival and building game inspired by Minecraft, made in Java. Explore an infinite block world, mine resources, build houses, travel to another dimension, and play with friends!

What's inside:

• Survival & Creative Modes: Play in Survival with health, hunger, fall damage, and underwater oxygen, or switch to Creative to fly around and build with unlimited blocks.

• Infinite World & Biomes: Explore Plains, Deserts, and Snowy Forests. Dig underground to find caves and mine coal, iron, gold, and diamonds.

• Villages & Trading: Find generated villages with houses, lamps, and wells, and trade your items for gold and resources with villagers.

• The Nether: Build an obsidian portal frame, light it up with flint and steel, and enter a dangerous dimension with netherrack, magma blocks, and glowing ceilings.

• Crafting, Smelting & Farming: Over 50 recipes! Craft wooden, stone, iron, and diamond tools, swords, and armor. Use furnaces to smelt ores and cook meat, store loot in chests, plant wheat seeds to make bread, and set your respawn point with a bed.

• Building & Blocks: Over 50 different blocks including colored concrete, terracotta, smooth quartz, glass, doors, trapdoors, bookshelves, lanterns, lamps, and chain-reaction TNT.

• Mobs & Combat: Survive against zombies that spawn at night and in dark caves, or hunt pigs, cows, sheep, and chickens for food and wool. Includes jump critical hits and optional PvP.

• Dynamic World & UI: Day/night cycle, rain and thunderstorms, glowing block lights, a real-time minimap, and 12 achievements to unlock.

• Custom Skins & Accounts: Load your own 64x64 PNG skin (or get an automatic one) and protect your nickname with a password.

• Multiplayer: Open your world for friends—everyone keeps their inventory, tools, and bed spawn points even after disconnecting!
